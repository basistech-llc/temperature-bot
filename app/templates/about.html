About

This application monitors and controls the BasisTech HVAC system, providing real-time visibility and control over your heating, cooling, and air quality systems

From the main dashboard, you can view current temperatures across all rooms and adjust fan speeds for HVAC units. Room-specific dashboards offer focused control for individual spaces like the Kitchen or Hickory room, showing temperature sensors and allowing you to fine-tune the climate for each area.

The system also displays local air quality information and weather conditions to help you make informed decisions about ventilation.

Show Admin Options

The Admin section provides additional tools for managing and monitoring the HVAC system:

  • Temperature Chart - View historical temperature and device data in graphical format
  • Air Quality Chart - View historical air quality (PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NO₂, CO, AQI) in graphical format
  • Alerts - Review system alerts and notifications from devices
  • Rules - Configure automated rules that control devices based on time, air quality, and other conditions
  • Today's Log - View detailed logs of all system activity for today
  • All Devices - Browse and inspect status for all connected devices

This project is open source and available on GitHub.
